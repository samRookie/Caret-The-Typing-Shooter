# Playtest Log — Futuristic Typing Shooter

This document logs external playtests conducted to evaluate the fairness, legibility, and engagement of the plain-visual core gameplay loop prior to visual and audio polish (Step 10).

---

## Playtest Protocol (Human-in-the-Loop)

1. **Recruit 3–5 testers**: Include at least one slow typist (< 30 WPM), medium typists (30–60 WPM), and fast typists (> 60 WPM).
2. **Briefing**: Provide only the single instruction:
   > *"Type the words on the ships before they reach you."*
   Do not explain locking, target switching, or Tab release up front.
3. **Observation**: Watch silently for at least 5 minutes per tester (screen-share or in-person). Do not coach until they ask; note what questions or confusion arise.
4. **Recording**: Complete one table row per tester immediately following their session.

## Pass / Fail Gate Thresholds (Section 9.C)

- **Target 1**: Median wave reached on `normal` by medium typists $\ge 3$.
- **Target 2**: $\ge 60\%$ understood target locking without coaching.
- **Target 3**: $\ge 60\%$ answered "Would play again: Y".
- **Target 4**: Zero label readability complaints repeated by two or more testers.
